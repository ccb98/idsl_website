About me

Professor of Intelligent Digital Systems · Head of iDSL · Director of Undergraduate Studies, Department of EEE

I am a Professor of Intelligent Digital Systems in Imperial College London's Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering. I lead iDSL and serve as the department's Director of Undergraduate Studies.

My research brings together the theory and practice of reconfigurable computing and design automation. I develop methods for designing, optimising and implementing digital systems around the computational needs of machine learning, computer vision and signal-processing algorithms. A central theme is co-optimising the algorithms and the architectures that run them, alongside using AI to make digital-system design more efficient.

Research interests

Intelligent systems and hardware

Co-designing algorithms and digital architectures so machine-learning and computer-vision systems can meet practical performance and energy constraints.

Reconfigurable computing

Methods and toolflows for mapping computational workloads onto FPGA and other reconfigurable platforms, including design automation and system-level optimisation.

AI for digital design

Exploring how AI can assist the design of circuits and systems. Through APRIL's Circuit & System Design pillar, this includes natural-language-driven workflows and AI-supported optimisation in electronic design automation.

Leadership and academic service

Department and research leadership

I have served as Director of Undergraduate Studies in the Department of EEE since 2021. Previously, I was Director of Postgraduate Studies from 2018 to 2021 and MSc Director for the MSc in Analogue and Digital Integrated Circuit Design from 2011 to 2018. I am also a Pillar 3 lead for APRIL, the UK AI Hub for Productive Research and Innovation in Electronics. Together with Professor Michael O'Boyle, I lead work on AI for circuit and system design, including AI-enabled design automation and more efficient design workflows.

Professional service

I have served on technical programme committees (TPCs) for international conferences including FCCM, FPL, FPT, DATE, SPPRA and VLSI-SoC. My programme leadership includes chairing the HiPEAC Workshop on Reconfigurable Computing, serving as Topic Chair for Reconfigurable Systems at DATE, and chairing FPL 2019. I continue to co-organise the HiPEAC Workshop on Reconfigurable Computing (WRC), which brings researchers together to discuss advances in reconfigurable architectures, tools and systems. Visit the WRC website for the workshop details. I also serve in editorial roles with journals including ACM Transactions on Reconfigurable Technology and Systems and the Journal of Systems Architecture.

In 2025, I was a joint recipient of Imperial EEE's Inspiring Contributions Award.

Consultancy and industry collaboration

I welcome conversations with organisations looking to solve challenging problems in intelligent digital systems. My expertise includes FPGA and reconfigurable computing, hardware acceleration for machine learning and computer vision, efficient deployment of AI systems, and AI-assisted digital design.

Engagements can range from an initial technical discussion or expert advice to a scoped collaborative project.
