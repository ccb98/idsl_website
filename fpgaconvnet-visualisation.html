Optimisation visualiser

See how the fpgaConvNet optimiser improves a design, step by step. The demonstration maps LeNet onto a ZedBoard FPGA.

← Back to fpgaConvNet · Original version: fpgaconvnet.com/visualisation (previous page).

To understand the architecture fpgaConvNet generates, and the optimisation steps taken to reach it, this page shows an interactive view of the design space exploration. Move the slider, or press Play, to see the state of the design at each step. The performance graph shows how throughput and latency change across the optimiser's steps, with the current point highlighted. The resource chart and the hardware block diagram update for the chosen point. You can zoom and drag the diagram.
