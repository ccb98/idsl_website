Optical Oscilloscope: real-time volumetric voltage imaging

Active

Wellcome · with the Department of Bioengineering

Imaging the electrical activity of neurons across a whole network of brain tissue, in three dimensions and in real time, using light-field microscopy. Scattering in brain tissue and the heavy computation behind the method are the main obstacles. We build optics-aware, computationally efficient deep neural networks that recover neuronal voltage signals from scattered light fields, and implement them on FPGAs and GPUs for kilohertz read-out suitable for closed-loop experiments.

  • FunderWellcome (£3.92M award)
  • PartnersBioengineering and UK DRI at Imperial; led by Dr Amanda Foust
  • Our roleProf. Christos Bouganis, co-investigator: neural networks on FPGAs and GPUs

HAMLET: human-centred generative AI for cultural industries

Active · 2025–2027

European Union · Horizon Europe, grant agreement 101178362

HAMLET makes generative AI tools available to cultural and creative organisations of any size, so they can take part in the digital transition. The project builds a collaboration hub where artists, designers, game developers and cultural professionals share expertise and resources, along with a suite of AI tools for areas such as 3D asset generation, adaptive storytelling and dance motion analysis. The tools are tested in pilot cases in several European countries.

  • FunderEuropean Union (EU contribution of about €3.9M)
  • Consortium12 organisations across Europe, including three from the UK, coordinated by a national research centre in Greece (CERTH)
