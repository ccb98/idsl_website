We are always looking for motivated, academically strong individuals whose interests relate to our research.

Research positions

We offer postdoctoral research positions for PhD holders and PhD studentships for strong students. Entry requirements: a strong academic record in digital system design, programming and mathematics; good knowledge of machine learning and image processing is desirable. Funding opportunities are announced in our news and on the department's website.
