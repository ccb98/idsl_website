Work with us

We are always looking for motivated, academically strong individuals whose interests relate to our research.

Research positions

We are always looking for strong students who want to do their PhD in our group. We can only consider applicants who hold an MSc or an MEng degree. Funding opportunities are announced in our news and on the department's website.

Before contacting us, please check the information below on the expectations for scholarships. Please do not email me enquiring about possible available funding. If there is funding available, it will be advertised here and on the appropriate channels.

Check current PhD opportunities and funding on the department website.

Undergraduate research

Hands-on research through final-year projects, industrial placements and summer placements.

Due to the high demand for research placements from Imperial College London students, we are currently unable to accommodate visiting or placement students from other institutions.

PhD application steps

1. Explore the opportunities

Review our research areas and current PhD opportunities, including any listed project details and closing dates.

2. Prepare your application

Please include your CV, a brief statement outlining your research interests, and why you believe you would be a good fit for the group.

3. Get in touch

Send your application materials and questions to Prof. Bouganis.

What we look for

  • A strong academic record in digital system design, programming and mathematics
  • Good knowledge of machine learning and image processing (desirable)
  • Motivation to work at the boundary of hardware and AI

Interested?

Departmental scholarships are available; however, these are highly competitive. Successful applicants typically demonstrate an excellent academic record and strong research potential, supported by evidence such as publications in relevant research areas.
