Optical Oscilloscope: real-time volumetric voltage imaging

Active

Wellcome · with the Department of Bioengineering

Imaging the electrical activity of neurons across a whole network of brain tissue, in three dimensions and in real time, using light-field microscopy. Scattering in brain tissue and the heavy computation behind the method are the main obstacles. We build optics-aware, computationally efficient deep neural networks that recover neuronal voltage signals from scattered light fields, and implement them on FPGAs and GPUs for kilohertz read-out suitable for closed-loop experiments.

  • FunderWellcome (£3.92M award)
  • PartnersBioengineering and UK DRI at Imperial; led by Dr Amanda Foust
  • Our roleProf. Christos Bouganis, co-investigator: neural networks on FPGAs and GPUs
